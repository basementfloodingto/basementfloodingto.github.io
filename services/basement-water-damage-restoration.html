What causes basement flooding in Toronto and how can it be prevented?

Common causes include heavy rainfall, sewer backups, and foundation cracks, exacerbated by Toronto's climate. Prevention involves proper drainage, sump pumps, and regular maintenance. Our Basement Water Damage Restoration team can assess and recommend solutions during service.
